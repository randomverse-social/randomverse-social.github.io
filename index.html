What is Randomverse?

A social experience built around discovery.

Randomverse is a discovery-first social experience built around one simple idea: every post deserves a chance to be discovered.

The idea

Open. Swipe. Discover.

No pressure to build a following. No need to compete for attention. Just a constantly changing window into moments, thoughts, images, and videos from people around the world.

The philosophy

Social, without expectations.

Privacy

Explore freely. Your data has a purpose.

Randomverse is designed with privacy in mind. You can explore the platform without creating an account, while the information we collect is used to operate, understand, and improve the experience.

01

Browse without an account

Explore Randomverse without signing up or creating a profile. An account is only needed for features such as posting, favorites, and profile customization.

02

Purposeful data use

We collect and use information that helps us operate Randomverse and understand how the platform is being used, including where our community is discovering it from.

03

Discovery stays about discovery

Your experience isn't built around your personal profile, popularity, or location. Randomverse is designed to keep the focus on discovering what you weren't expecting.
